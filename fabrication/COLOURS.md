# Part colours (physical model)

Rule: different parts get different colours; copies of the same part share one colour.
Parts glued into one piece share one colour. Moving links stand out from the frame and from each other.

Print colours are final (PETG, 0.2 mm layers). The disc is light blue on the dark blue base, and the rocker is yellow like the TT motor.
